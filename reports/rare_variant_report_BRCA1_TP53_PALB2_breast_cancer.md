# Rare Variant Information Report: BRCA1, TP53, and PALB2 in Breast Cancer

Data were aggregated from ClinVar, Ensembl, Open Targets, ClinicalTrials.gov, PubMed, Reactome, and ClinPGx to support biomarker-driven trial matching for breast cancer.

| 34780712 | Cancer incidence, patterns, and genotype-phenotype associations in individuals with pathogenic germline TP53 variants | Lancet Oncol | 2021 |

**Key findings from literature:**
- Protein-truncating variants in BRCA1, BRCA2, PALB2, ATM, and CHEK2 show significant association with breast cancer risk (PMID: 33471991)
- PALB2 truncating variants (OR=3.11; 95% CI 2.15-4.69) associated with increased breast cancer risk (PMID: 28779002)
- TP53 germline variants result in ~24-fold higher cancer incidence compared to general population (PMID: 34780712)
- Missense variants in BRCA1/BRCA2 show elevated risk in functional protein domains (FAT, PIK, RING, BRCT domains) (PMID: 35585550)
